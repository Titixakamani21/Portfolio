THE CHALLENGE

A premium brand in a market full of generic noise.

A new Indian herbal soap brand needed everything built from scratch. The goal was to launch as silent luxury in a crowded mass market.

BRAND STRATEGY

Every decision was intentional.

Here is the thinking that shaped the brand from the ground up.

01

Positioning

Silent luxury rooted in Indian heritage. Not rustic. Not clinical. The premium space no competitor occupied.

02

Audience

Women 22 to 45 buying for themselves and gifting occasions. Skincare conscious. Values ingredients over marketing.

03

Brand Voice

Calm, sophisticated, never loud. Luxury brands do not shout. Every word on the site was written to this standard.
